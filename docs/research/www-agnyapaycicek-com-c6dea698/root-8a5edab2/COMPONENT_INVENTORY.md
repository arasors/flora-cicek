# Component Inventory

1. `SiteHeader` — sticky global navigation and mobile disclosure.
2. `HeroSection` — marketing copy, two CTAs, hero image.
3. `ServicesHighlightSection` — three detailed service cards.
4. `ValueCtaSection` — pale-green centered value proposition.
5. `StatsSection` — three business metrics.
6. `StorySection` — company history and inline links.
7. `AllServicesSection` — fourteen compact service cards.
8. `ReferenceLogosSection` — duplicated, animated client-logo marquee.
9. `InspirationCarouselSection` — twenty-four image carousel.
10. `ProjectsGridSection` — twelve-image portfolio grid.
11. `FinalCtaSection` — three benefit cards and contact CTA.
12. `SiteFooter` — brand, service/navigation/contact columns and copyright.
13. `WhatsAppFab` — fixed contact control.

The service, logo, inspiration, and project collections are supplied from a namespaced data module. SVG icons are either shared site components or Lucide primitives matching the source inline SVGs.
